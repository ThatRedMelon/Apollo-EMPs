Replicates the behaviour of P23 as it were on Apollo 7.

### Procedures

- Verify N26 has been loaded successfully
- Enter V30E
- Verify P23 is displayed
- F 59 - Perform optics SLOS/LLOS calibration
- F 06 87 - Verify trunnion bias is correct
- F 51 - Perform sextant mark - align star to correct point on horizon
- F 06 87 - Mark optics angles
- F 06 34 - Mark time
- F 50 25 - Perform additional sightings?
- Proceed to go to P00, enter to restart program
